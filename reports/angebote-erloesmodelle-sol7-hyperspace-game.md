## Executive Summary
SOL-7 is a browser-first (HTML5 Canvas + exportable Blender assets), educational-artistic interactive game/simulator featuring a "Happy Space Cat" piloting a hyperspace-capable ship. Core mechanics revolve around warp management (1-7× multipliers), resource (Mission Credits) economy, and actions: Thrust, Compulsion, Prism, Observe Wave. 

**Ties to Real Spaceship Scenarios** (grounded in physics prototypes):
- Alcubierre metric warp bubbles (theoretical faster-than-light via spacetime contraction/expansion).
- DWARF thrusters (directed high-efficiency propulsion; optimized in Blizzwar physics sims for Mars/Moon/New Earth round-trips).
- Real mission analogs: SpaceX-style resupply, Mars transit delta-v, time dilation on long voyages, plasma/warp field stability, gravity assists.
- Educational overlays: Tooltips, sliders, and optimizers demonstrating real formulas (e.g., warp energy scaling, relativity effects on "cat crew" clocks).

**NS Puzzle (Nexus Shift Puzzle) – Unique Differentiator**:
The signature puzzle mechanic: Players manipulate "Crystal Prisms" and "Compulsion Fields" to solve Nexus Shift challenges. These are dynamic, physics-constrained optimization puzzles:
- Stabilize warp bubbles under simulated energy/matter constraints (real Alcubierre-inspired math).
- Optimize trajectories for minimal time/fuel in multi-body scenarios (Mars, asteroids, hyperspace lanes).
- Prism refraction puzzles for signal/comms in distorted spacetime (ties to real deep-space comms challenges).
- Progressive difficulty: From simple Earth-orbit shifts to interstellar "blowup lab" stress tests (ns-blowup-lab heritage).
This blends cute cat aesthetics with hard sci-fi problem-solving, exportable to real engineering tools or classroom use. No other game combines adorable protagonist + verifiable physics puzzle depth.
